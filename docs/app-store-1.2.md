# App Store Connect — version 1.2

## Description

Split The G combines a private pub notebook, venue discovery, and still-photo analysis of Guinness presentation.

YOUR PRIVATE PUB NOTEBOOK
Save places you want to visit and remember the details that matter to you. Add venues from the directory or create your own entries with a name and address. Organize places as Want to visit or Visited, write private notes, and add personal tags for food, outdoor seating, atmosphere, or access. Search your saved places and open directions when you are ready to go.

The Notebook works without an account. Guest notes stay on your device and can be used offline. Optional sign-in opens a separate account Notebook that syncs between devices; guest entries are not transferred automatically. Tags are your personal observations, not verified venue information.

DISCOVER VENUES
Browse the pub directory and interactive map, view available addresses and opening information, explore community photos, and save places to your Notebook.

ANALYZE A STILL PHOTO
Take or choose a photo of a Guinness glass. Computer vision detects the glass, G mark, and visible liquid line, then returns an annotated result and a 0–5 visual-alignment measurement. You can analyze an existing image; drinking is not required. Image analysis needs an internet connection and a clear view of the G mark.

KEEP YOUR ANALYSIS HISTORY
Sign in with Apple or Google to associate analyses with your account and revisit them from Profile. Record optional venue and price details with an analysis.

Community photo reporting and user blocking are available in the app. You can delete your account from account settings.

For adults of legal drinking age. Please enjoy alcohol responsibly.

## App Review notes

Version 1.2 follows the rejection of version 1.0 (3) under Guideline 4.3(b). The shipped app now includes a functional private venue Notebook and still-image analysis, with the following changes from the reviewed version:

- The former Compete tab and competitive rankings have been removed from the app's navigation. The app does not provide timed drinking challenges, multiplayer drinking games, or rewards for consuming alcohol.
- Notebook is a private venue-planning tool. Users can add custom places, save directory venues, mark Want to visit or Visited, write private notes, apply personal observation tags, search/filter entries, open directions, and remove saved places. These functions work without sign-in; the guest Notebook is stored locally and works offline. Signed-in users have a separate account Notebook with private sync.
- Pubs provides venue discovery, an interactive map, available opening information, and community photographs. Feed is a chronological photo gallery, with reporting and blocking controls.
- Still-image analysis remains available and is disclosed in the store description. It detects the visible G mark and liquid line and returns an annotated 0–5 visual-alignment result. An existing photograph can be used; the app never asks the user to drink before analyzing an image.
- Profile contains personal analysis history and optional recorded price information. Notebook is not an analysis leaderboard.
- Version 1.2 also improves photo upload preparation, iOS launch compatibility, data preloading, and first-load skeletons. Existing content stays visible during background refreshes.

Suggested review steps (no account required):
1. Open Notebook from the bottom navigation. Use + to add a place with a name and optional address. Add a note, choose a visit status and tags, and test search/filtering. Guest entries remain after relaunch.
2. Open Pubs, choose a listed venue, and save it to Notebook. Return to Notebook to view the saved venue and open its details or directions.
3. Use the center navigation button to open the analyzer. Choose Upload a photo and select a clear existing photograph showing a Guinness glass and its G mark. The service returns an annotated measurement. A photograph without a detectable G produces guidance to choose another image.
4. Open a community photo to find the reporting and user-blocking controls.

Apple/Google sign-in is optional for account sync and personal analysis history. Account deletion is in Profile > account settings. No subscription or purchase is required for these features.
